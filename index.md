# RemyMoCo Higgins

## Private AI Personal Assistant

RemyMoCo Higgins is a privately operated AI assistant designed to help its owner manage emails, documents, tasks and reminders.

### What Higgins does

- Monitors a dedicated Gmail account.
- Processes emails forwarded by its authorised owner.
- Summarises emails and documents using AI.
- Sends notifications and reminders through Telegram.
- Prepares outgoing emails that require the owner's approval before sending.

### Privacy and security

Higgins uses Google OAuth 2.0 for Gmail access. The assistant primarily operates on the owner's Windows computer and uses Google Gemini and Telegram for selected AI and communication functions.
